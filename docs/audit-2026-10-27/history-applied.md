## Notes for the owners

- The daily-game notes (`dailyContext.what` and `today`) are drawn by `WhoIsWho` in `src/components/daily/Matchup.tsx` (line 315) whether or not the question has been answered, so they show before the answer, like the region notes. A few existing `what` lines still contain size superlatives that hint at the answer: Russian Empire ("the third-largest empire in history"), Inca ("The largest empire in the Americas"), Polish–Lithuanian Commonwealth ("one of Europe's largest states") and Vijayanagara (its capital "one of the world's largest cities").
