# Border Drift: history copy fact-check

Scope: `src/content/daily-context.ts` (120 entries), `src/content/era-intros.ts` (16 intros), `src/content/lineages.ts` (19 lineages), `src/content/region-context.ts` (136 notes). Read only; nothing in the repo was edited.

Method:
- I checked every claim against web sources (Britannica, university and museum pages, UN and official pages, and well-sourced Wikipedia articles, with their cited scholars named where it matters).
- To check the "today" lists I sampled points inside every game shape at its snapshot year (`public/data/eras/<era>.json`, matched to the `sub` year in `public/data/daily/index.json`) and tested each point against the present-day borders (`eras/present.json`). This gives the share of each shape's land in each modern country. The script is in the scratchpad (`scripts/overlap.mjs`) and its output is in `overlap.txt`.
- Where the map shape itself is historically wrong but the copy is right, the finding is listed separately in section B. That is a map-data problem, not a copy problem.

Types: [WRONG] = factually incorrect or incomplete in a way that misleads. [VAGUE] = leaves out or softens a well-documented atrocity, slave trade or sovereignty fact that the owners' rule asks for. [DISPUTED] = contested point that needs both views. [STYLE] = consistency, wording, or size-word rule.
