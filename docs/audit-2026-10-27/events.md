# Fact-check: src/content/events.ts (timeline, "times land changed hands")

Audit date: 2026-10-27. Read-only review: nothing in the repo was edited.
Scope: all 119 events (dates, who took or lost what, areas and numbers, wording, contested claims, BCE/CE style).
Standard applied: the owners' rule ("Extremely historically accurate, even if it upsets people. The whole truth, not vague.").
Line numbers point at the `summary:` line unless the issue is in the title or a year field.

Types: [WRONG] = factual error. [VAGUE] = euphemism or an omitted conquest, massacre, expulsion or genocide central to the event. [DISPUTED] = contested point stated as settled, or one side's claim missing. [STYLE] = consistency.

Suggested text is a proposal: numbers are attributed to their source, as the rule requires. Where sources give a range, the range is given.
