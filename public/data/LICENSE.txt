Map data licenses for the files in this folder
==============================================

eras/*.json (2000 BCE to 1783, and 1900 to 1960), polities.json, rulers-grid.json,
era-stats.json, and the historical shapes in daily/ (daily/index.json and daily/shapes/*)
  Derived from historical-basemaps by André Ourednik and contributors
  https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps
  License: GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)
  Full text: COPYING.txt in this folder, or https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html

  Changes made for this site: simplified and quantized outlines, renamed or
  re-assigned rulers where the source was wrong for its year, filled unmapped
  land, split a few shapes and cut single places out for the daily challenge.
  Every change is listed at /about and in src/content/data-corrections.json.
  These derived files are distributed under the same GPL-3.0 license.

  Source: the scripts that build these files (scripts/build-data.mjs,
  scripts/build-daily.mjs, scripts/lib/) and the corrections
  (src/content/data-corrections.json) are published at
  https://github.com/AnthonyTFM/scale-shift and are offered under GPL-3.0.

eras/1800.json, eras/1815.json, eras/1878.json and eras/1880.json, and their entries
in polities.json, rulers-grid.json and era-stats.json
  Cliopatria (v0.2.1), Seshat: Global History Databank,
  https://github.com/Seshat-Global-History-Databank/cliopatria
  Bennett, J. S. et al. (2025). Cliopatria - A geospatial database of world-wide
  political entities from 3400BCE to 2024CE. Scientific Data.
  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-04516-9
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes made for this site: clipped to Natural Earth's coastline, simplified
  and quantized; OpenHistoricalMap's and HistoGIS's borders (below) laid over it;
  some polities and rulers renamed where it was wrong for the year (listed at
  /about and in src/content/data-corrections.json); a few places redrawn from
  Natural Earth's outlines or approximately (listed in scripts/build-data.mjs).
  These maps use no historical-basemaps data.

Europe in eras/1815.json, 1878.json, 1880.json, 1900.json and 1914.json
  HistoGIS, Austrian Centre for Digital Humanities and Cultural Heritage (ACDH-CH),
  Austrian Academy of Sciences, https://histogis.acdh.oeaw.ac.at/
  License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes made for this site: clipped to Natural Earth's coastline, simplified
  and quantized, merged with the rest of each map.

Europe in eras/1920.json, 1930.json, 1938.json, 1945.json and 1960.json
(plus French Morocco, the Hatay State and Manchukuo), and most states in
eras/1800.json, 1815.json, 1878.json and 1880.json
  OpenHistoricalMap contributors, https://www.openhistoricalmap.org/
  License: public domain dedication (CC0 1.0),
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
  Changes made for this site: simplified, clipped to Natural Earth's coastline,
  quantized and merged with the rest of each map.

eras/1994.json, eras/2000.json, eras/2010.json, eras/present.json, us-states.json,
lakes.json, globe.json and the present-day shapes in daily/, plus the outlines used
to give land back to the right country on the maps from 1800 to 1960
  Natural Earth (public domain), https://www.naturalearthdata.com/
  US states: US Census Bureau via us-atlas (public domain)
  The site's /world.svg and /globe-still.svg images are drawn from the same
  Natural Earth outlines.

antarctic-claims.json
  Outlines cut from Natural Earth (public domain). The claim lines and years
  were written for this site from the published claims of each country.

Population figures: World Bank, World Development Indicators (CC BY 4.0),
https://data.worldbank.org/
